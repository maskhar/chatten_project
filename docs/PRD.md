# Chatten Cafe Digital Platform

## Product
Destination website and management dashboard for Chatten Cafe. Public goals: inspire visits, explain menu and experiences, increase map and WhatsApp actions, and improve SEO. Admin goals: manage content, menu, gallery, promotions/events, business details, SEO, and media.

## Acceptance criteria
- Public published content is readable without CMS write access.
- Authorized staff manage CMS content in later phases.
- Website uses self-hosted Supabase only.
- Content, media metadata, and SEO are database-managed.

## Assumptions
Business copy, hours, address, booking channel, and imagery remain placeholders until confirmed.
